# Issue P2-017: a stop-limit order cancelled before activation was labelled "Activated" in My Orders

- **Date found:** 2026-11-08, in the owner's real MetaMask run of Bonus 3 (P2-8B.4 follow-up). Fixed the same day in P2-8C.
- **Phase:** introduced in P2-8B.4 (`web/components/trade/my-orders.tsx`, the stop-state line). **Model that wrote the original code:** Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Medium effort requested).
- **Found by:** the human (the owner cancelled order #3 and read the history line), then confirmed against the chain by the AI with read-only calls. Not found by any of the 79 new unit tests, the 11 real-chain tests or the 17 browser tests of P2-8B.4. Severity: **Low** (a misleading label). No funds, escrow, refund or contract behaviour were affected: the order status and the 17 SGDx refund were correct.

## 1. Why the AI made the mistake
The AI modelled a stop order as two states, *pending* (`status == 4`) and *activated* (everything else), because `getOrder` reports `PendingTrigger` only until the order activates. It overlooked that a *pending* stop can also leave `PendingTrigger` by being **cancelled by its owner** (`cancelOrder` accepts status 4), which also ends in `Cancelled` without ever activating. `getOrder` cannot tell that apart from "activated, then cancelled". The code inferred a past event (activation) from the current status instead of reading the event that records it (`StopOrderTriggered`). The tests did not catch it because none of them cancelled a *pending* stop and then asserted the wording of its history line: the cancel tests asserted the refund and that the row stayed in the list, and the activation tests used orders that really had activated.

## 2. Why it is problematic
For order #3 (a buy stop: trigger 1.8, limit 1.7, 10 NTU, escrow 17 SGDx, cancelled by its owner in block 22) the page said *"Activated: it is now an ordinary order at its limit price (cancelled)."* The chain says otherwise: placement emitted `StopOrderPlaced`, cancellation emitted `OrderCancelled` with a 17 SGDx refund, and **no `StopOrderTriggered` exists for #3** anywhere in the chain's history. A trader could conclude their stop had triggered, and that it had been exposed to the market, when it never was. It also blurs the difference between an owner cancel and the engine's own auto-cancel at activation. It is a wrong factual statement about an on-chain history in a financial interface, and it appeared in the screenshots and video material.

## 3. How we fixed it
- `classifyStop` in `web/lib/stop-history.ts` (pure, unit tested) decides the wording from the order's status **and** its history records: "activated" is claimed only when a `StopOrderTriggered` record exists; a cancelled stop with a *complete* history read and no trigger record is **"Cancelled before activation"**; trigger plus cancel in the **same transaction** is the engine's auto-cancel; trigger plus a cancel in a **later** transaction is the owner cancelling after activation; an unreadable history gives neutral wording and never says "Activated".
- `readStopHistory` makes one `eth_getLogs` for both events, filtered on the indexed order ids and bounded from the stops' placement block; `useStopHistory` runs it only when a non-pending stop is listed, caches per (order, status) set, is skipped by the per-block refresh, and degrades to neutral wording on failure.
- My Orders now also shows the Trigger Price and the Limit Price of every stop for its whole life, and uses a status badge per on-chain status.

## 4. Evidence the fix works
**Before** (the pre-fix build still serving the Bonus 3 evidence chain on port 3101, driven read-only with the mock wallet as Anvil account 5, no transaction sent; the DOM text, and `docs/ai-audit/test-evidence/person2/p2-8c-screens/my-orders-cancelled-stop-BEFORE.jpg`):
```
#3  NTU / SGDx  Buy  1.7 ... Cancelled  Stop-limit: trigger 1.8 SGDx, limit 1.7 SGDx. Activated: it is now an ordinary order at its limit price (cancelled).
```
and the chain (read-only): `StopOrderTriggered` logs in the whole history of the market: one, block 35, order #4; none for order #3.
**The unit tests** (`web/lib/stop-history.test.ts`, 15 tests) were written first and failed because the module did not exist. As a non-vacuity check the old rule was re-introduced (every non-pending stop reported as activated): **4 tests failed** (the order #3 regression, the missing-cancel-record case, the inconsistent-history case and the "never says activated without a trigger record" property); treating a same-transaction cancel as an owner cancel failed 1 more. Both mutants were reverted and the file restored byte-identical.
**After:** `lib/stop-history.test.ts` 15 passed; the real-chain test in `lib/stop.integration.test.ts` (cancelled before activation, activated and filled, activated then cancelled by the owner, the engine's auto-cancel in a 101-order book, one request, an RPC failure, a bounded block range) passes; the browser case "REGRESSION (order #3 of the Bonus 3 MetaMask run)" (`web/e2e/ux.e2e.mjs`) cancels a buy stop through the UI and asserts *"You cancelled it before it activated. It never triggered…"*, that "Activated" never appears, and that the Trigger Price is still shown: it passed (19 of 19, mock wallet, scratch chain 8548). A history read that fails (the request is made to fail in the browser test) shows *"Cancelled. Whether it had activated first could not be read, so no claim is made."* and the list stays intact.

**What this does not show:** it was fixed and verified on a scratch chain with a mock wallet. The owner's MetaMask journey recorded the defect, not its fix; the fix has not been looked at in real MetaMask.
