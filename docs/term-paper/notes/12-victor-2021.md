## 4. Limits for an on-chain order book
- IDEX and EtherDelta keep order books off-chain (p. 2); matching rules and the IDEX operator are not like our fully on-chain engine.
- Measures volume inflation, not stop-trigger manipulation or price impact; the paper does not study effect on prices (listed as future work, p. 10).
- Period 2017 to 2020 on PoW Ethereum; activity "virtually" stopped on both DEXs after mid-2018 / mid-2019 (p. 8).
- Lower bound only; detection misses wash trades outside the parameters (p. 9).
- USD values use daily ETH/USD rates from Etherscan (p. 3).

## 5. Claim check
- Summary given: "over 159 million USD of wash trades on IDEX and EtherDelta; self-trades common and easy to prevent."
- Verdict: partly supported
- Evidence:
  - "over 159 million USD": holds approximately. Abstract says "159 million U.S. Dollars" (p. 1); Sec. 6.5 says "a total of $159 million" (p. 9); conclusion says "in excess of 159 million U.S. Dollars" (p. 10). Table 2 sums to 83,531,254 + 75,846,518 = 159,377,772 USD. Window 02/09/2017 to 05/04/2020; it is a conservative lower bound.
  - "on IDEX and EtherDelta": holds (p. 1, Table 2).
  - "self-trades common": holds for EtherDelta (vast majority of wash trades, 58,791 self-trades, p. 7, p. 9); on IDEX "also common" but "only account for a small share" of wash trades (p. 7, p. 8 to 9). Self-trades are 0.53% and 1.09% of all trades, so "common" means common among wash trades, not among all trades.
  - "easy to prevent": the paper does say self-trades "could easily be prevented" (p. 7, p. 10), but also that other topologies "can circumvent" self-trade prevention (p. 9, Sec. 7.1), and IDEX's claimed prevention did not stop self-trades. So "easy to prevent" holds for single-account self-trades only, not for wash trading in general.
