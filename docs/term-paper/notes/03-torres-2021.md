## 4. Limits for an on-chain order book
- Insertion detection is limited to AMM DEXes (Bancor, Uniswap V1/V2, SushiSwap) (p. 10); the paper says it knew of no other insertion use case (p. 6, Sec. 4.3). No on-chain limit order book measured.
- Ethereum L1 mainnet only, PoW era, up to Nov 2020 (p. 8, Sec. 5.1); before EIP-1559 and Flashbots-style private bundles (not discussed in the file).
- Displacement detection only covers bot-contract attacks within 100 blocks (p. 6); suppression only multi-block, multi-transaction patterns (p. 8).
- Printed internal inconsistencies to avoid quoting: 2,673 cases printed as 0.01% direct insertion attacks (p. 11), but 2,673 / 196,691 is about 1.36% (my arithmetic); average profit per insertion attack printed as 78.72 USD in text (p. 11) vs Table 3 mean 65.05 USD (p. 10); insertion accumulated profit printed as over 13.9M USD (p. 11) vs Table 3 mean x count, about 12.79M USD (my arithmetic). Per-attack means x counts across all three types give about 18.42M USD, consistent with the headline 18.41M (my arithmetic).

## 5. Claim check
- Summary given: "over 11 million blocks, about 200 thousand attacks, 18.41 million USD attacker profit."
- Verdict: supported
- Evidence:
  - "over 11 million blocks": holds. Abstract: more than 11M blocks (p. 1); exact scope: the first 11,300,000 blocks, July 30, 2015 to November 21, 2020 (p. 8, Sec. 5.1).
  - "about 200 thousand attacks": holds. Abstract: almost 200K attacks (p. 1); exact 199,725 (p. 2, p. 16). Component counts sum to 199,724 (see F2).
  - "18.41 million USD attacker profit": holds. Abstract: accumulated profit of 18.41M USD (p. 1); over 18.41M USD (p. 2, p. 16). It is a net figure that includes loss-making attacks (p. 11) and is a lower bound (p. 8, Sec. 5.2).
  - Time window should be stated with the numbers: Ethereum mainnet, Jul 2015 to Nov 2020.
