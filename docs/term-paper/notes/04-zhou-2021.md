## 4. Limits for an on-chain order book
- AMM-only (constant product, Uniswap V1 era); limit order books explicitly excluded (p. 1, Sec. I).
- Empirical attack runs only on the authors' own transactions in one tiny market (ETH/VERI, 3.50 USD liquidity) (p. 8); revenue figures for real traffic are estimates, not observed attacks (p. 9, Table III).
- Ethereum L1, PoW, pre-EIP-1559 gas price ordering (p. 10); block forks and margin trading not modelled (p. 5, Sec. III-E).
- No stop orders, fill caps, or dust orders; the "minimum profitable victim input" logic depends on AMM price impact, which a CLOB at maker price does not have in the same form.
- Text extraction note: Table III row values "602, 85" and "196, 72" (p. 9) are garbled; the provider rows read 60,285 and 19,672, and these with 25,204 sum to the printed total 105,161 (my arithmetic). Not checked against the rendered PDF (page rendering tool unavailable).

## 5. Claim check
- Summary given: "first formal model of sandwich attacks, on AMMs."
- Verdict: partly supported
- Evidence:
  - The paper's own wording is a hedged priority claim about formalizing and quantifying, not about a "formal model": "To the best of our knowledge, we are the first to formalize and quantify sandwich attacks." (p. 1, Sec. I, "This paper"). The abstract says the authors formalize, analytically exposit and empirically evaluate sandwich attacks (p. 1).
  - "on AMMs": holds. Scope restricted to AMM DEXs, not limit order books (p. 1, Sec. I); model is the constant product AMM (p. 3, Sec. II-B).
  - "first": only the authors' own "to the best of our knowledge" claim; not independently established. The same front-and-back pattern was described earlier, informally, as insertion by Eskandari et al. 2019 with an order-book example (02 file p. 4, Sec. 3), and Torres et al. note that insertion is also called a sandwich attack (03 file p. 4, Sec. 3.2). Zhou et al. also cite a prior Bancor front-running implementation [4], which their taker attack section says it formalizes (p. 4, Sec. III-C).
  - Version caveat: wording checked in arXiv v1 only; S&P 2021 text not checked.
  - Suggested safe phrasing for our paper: Zhou et al. claim to be the first to formalize and quantify sandwich attacks, for AMM DEXs [04].
  - Related caution: Torres et al. describe Zhou et al. as estimating theoretical profit without backing it with real observed data (03 file p. 16, Sec. 7). That is about attacks in the wild; Zhou et al. did run 40 mainnet experiments on their own transactions (p. 8 to 9, Sec. V).
