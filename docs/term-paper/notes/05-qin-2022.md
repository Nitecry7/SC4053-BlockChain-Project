## 4. Limits for an on-chain order book
- All extraction measured is on AMMs (Uniswap V1/V2/V3, Sushiswap, Curve, Swerve, 1inch, Bancor) and lending protocols; no order-book DEX is measured (p. 4, Sec. IV).
- PoW Ethereum L1 mainnet only; mempool, gas-price auctions and miner forking thresholds are PoW-era (p. 2, Sec. II-A; p. 12, Sec. VII).
- Heuristic estimates without ground truth (false positives and negatives acknowledged) (p. 7, Sec. IV-E).
- Sandwich profit relies on AMM slippage tolerance; in a limit order book the victim's limit price bounds the loss, so magnitude does not transfer.
- Relayer analysis is about Flashbots-era PoW relays; post-Merge PBS is not covered (not found in the paper).

## 5. Claim check
- Summary given: "quantifies value from sandwiches, liquidations and arbitrage; links it to consensus risk."
- Verdict: supported
- Evidence: Quantification: 540.54M USD over 32 months (1 Dec 2018 to 5 Aug 2021) from exactly these three sources, with 174.34M (sandwich), 89.18M (liquidations, over 28 months, from inception to block 12965000) and 277.02M (arbitrage) (p. 1 Abstract and Fig. 1; Sec. IV, pp. 4 to 6). Consensus link: abstract states BEV "was quantitatively shown to deteriorate the blockchain's consensus security" and a 10% miner forks if BEV exceeds 4x block reward (p. 1); Sec. VII counts at least 2,407 blocks above that threshold and derives a forking threshold curve (p. 12, Fig. 13); Sec. VI argues relayers aggravate consensus attacks via revenue concentration (p. 11 to 12). Nuances: the paper uses the term BEV (treating MEV as a subset, p. 3, Sec. III-B), also adds a fourth contribution (transaction replay, 35.37M USD) and a relayer model that the summary does not mention. The forking threshold model is borrowed from prior work [8], [11] (p. 12).
